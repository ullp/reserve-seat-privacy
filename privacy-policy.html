Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 14, 2026

Introduction

Park & Seat ("we", "us", or "our") operates the Park & Seat mobile application (the "Service").

This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our Service and the choices you have associated with that data.

Information We Collect

Personal Information

When you register for an account or use our Service, we may collect the following information:

  • Name: Your full name for account identification
  • Email Address: Used for account registration, login, and communication
  • Password: Securely stored for account authentication
  • User Role: Whether you are an admin or regular user

Usage Data

We may also collect information about how the Service is accessed and used ("Usage Data"). This Usage Data may include:

  • Device Information: Information about the device you use to access the Service
  • Log Data: Information about your interactions with the Service, including access times and features used

Booking Information

When you make reservations through our Service, we collect:

  • Office Selection: Which office location you are booking for
  • Resource Type: Whether you're booking a seat or parking space
  • Resource Details: Specific seat number, room name, or parking spot
  • Booking Times: Start and end times for your reservations
  • Booking History: Your past and current reservations

Subscription and Purchase Information

If you subscribe to a premium plan, we additionally process the plan (monthly or annual), a purchase token issued by the store or by our backend, the purchase and expiry dates, and whether the subscription was cancelled.

We never receive or store your card number, bank details or store account password. Payments are processed by Apple (App Store / StoreKit) or Google (Google Play Billing) under their own privacy policies; we only receive a purchase token and the resulting entitlement status.

How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for various purposes:

  • Account Management: To create and manage your user account
  • Authentication: To verify your identity and provide secure access
  • Service Functionality: To enable seat and parking space reservations
  • User Management: For administrators to manage users and office configurations
  • Communication: To send important notifications about your account or the Service
  • Service Improvement: To analyze usage patterns and improve our Service

Data Storage and Security

Storage Location

Your personal data is stored in a managed PostgreSQL database hosted by Supabase (see "Third-Party Services and Processors" below). Data is transmitted over TLS. Some data (your name, e-mail, office layout and your own bookings) is also cached in your browser's LocalStorage so the app can work offline; this cache is cleared when you sign out.

Data Retention

Data Retention period
Account (name, e-mail, role, verification state) Until you or an administrator deletes the account
Bookings 24 months from the booking date, then deleted
Subscription and purchase records While the subscription exists, then as required by tax and accounting law
Browser LocalStorage cache Cleared on sign-out

Backups are kept for up to 30 days and are purged on a rolling basis.

Security Measures

We implement appropriate security measures to protect against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction of your personal information. These measures include:

  • Secure authentication protocols
  • Encrypted data transmission
  • Access controls and monitoring

Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your Personal Information to third parties, except in the following circumstances:

With Your Consent

We may share your information when we have your explicit consent to do so.

For Legal Reasons

We may disclose your information if required to do so by law or in response to valid requests by public authorities.

Business Transfers

If Park & Seat is involved in a merger, acquisition, or asset sale, your Personal Data may be transferred. We will provide notice before your personal information is transferred and becomes subject to a different privacy policy.

Your Data Protection Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: Request copies of your personal data
  • Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Erasure: Request deletion of your personal data
  • Restriction: Request restriction of processing your personal data
  • Objection: Object to processing of your personal data
  • Portability: Request transfer of your data to another organization

To exercise these rights, please contact us at ullmann.web@gmail.com. We respond within 30 days.

How to delete your data

  1. In the app: an administrator can delete a user under Admin → Users, which removes the account row and its bookings.
  2. By e-mail: send a deletion request to ullmann.web@gmail.com from the address you registered with. We confirm receipt, delete the account, its bookings and its subscription records, and reply once the deletion is complete (within 30 days).

Bookings are otherwise deleted automatically 24 months after the booking date.
